# User study execution sheet — planned, not conducted

## Goal and boundaries

Test whether bandleaders can identify a change, prepare clarification questions and produce a reviewed terms sheet with less active work than their ordinary messenger + notes/template or general-chatbot workflow. The **30% reduction** is a target for this narrowed process only; it is not a measured result. This is a small exploratory usability study, not proof of New York demand, legal compliance or willingness to pay.

The user may voluntarily invite 2–3 musicians they know. Do not automatically send messages, contact participants or publish anything. Provide the user with suggested wording only when requested; the participant may decline or stop. Describe the current product as a prototype and never ask anyone to use real private conversations, contract data, names, addresses, emails, passwords or OTPs.

Use only the fictional cases below. Before any live session, verify the selected prototype path in a fresh browser, including access, safe saving and the applicable inference budget. If live AI is unavailable, explicitly label a fixture-only walkthrough; it cannot establish AI time savings. Use any screen sharing or recording only with explicit participant permission; no recording is needed for this protocol.

## Participants and assignment

- Record anonymous IDs such as P01, P02; keep identity/contact details outside this public repository and out of test records.
- Describe only necessary role/familiarity information (for example, “bandleader; first time using product”), not employer, location, protected characteristics or other identifying details.
- Obtain verbal agreement to the task and anonymous notes. Participation is voluntary; a participant may stop without explanation. Do not collect real gig details to make the study more realistic.
- Use both workflows for each participant with different but similarly difficult cases. Alternate order as far as the small sample allows: P01 baseline A then product B; P02 product A then baseline B; P03 baseline B then product A.
- Give each participant a brief, equal orientation to both workflows **before** the timed tasks. Record familiarity and orientation time separately. Do not walk them through the solution during timing; record any assistance.

## Equivalent synthetic cases

Each task has an original note, a later request and a prepared fictional reply. Give the reply only after the participant has written their clarification questions; there is no real counterparty to message. Both require one time change, one extra set, fee clarification, an explicit reply and an unresolved payment date. All text below is fictional.

### Case A — Blue Alley

Initial: “Venue: Blue Alley offers two sets on November 20, 2026, at 20:00 and 22:00 for a total performance fee of $900. Musician: Those date, times and fee work for me.”

Request: “Venue: Could we start at 21:00 and add another set? Musician: I will check. What would the total fee and the set times be?”

Prepared reply: “Venue: For three sets at 21:00, 22:00 and 23:00, the total performance fee would be $1,200. Musician: Those times and the $1,200 total work for me. Venue: I will confirm the payment date later.”

### Case B — Copper Room

Initial: “Venue: Copper Room offers two sets on December 12, 2026, at 19:00 and 21:00 for a total performance fee of $800. Musician: Those date, times and fee work for me.”

Request: “Venue: Could we start at 20:00 and add another set? Musician: I will check. What would the total fee and the set times be?”

Prepared reply: “Venue: For three sets at 20:00, 21:00 and 22:00, the total performance fee would be $1,100. Musician: Those times and the $1,100 total work for me. Venue: I will confirm the payment date later.”

## Moderator instructions

1. Explain: “Treat this as a fictional gig. Review the original conditions, identify the request and write what you need to clarify. Do not send any message.” Start the active-work timer when the participant begins reading or entering the source.
2. Record whether they treat the request as pending, retain the original terms, and ask for the full new schedule and total fee. The original fee must not automatically carry to the proposed extra set.
3. When questions are complete, pause the active timer and provide the prepared reply. Record waiting or moderator time separately. Resume the timer when the participant starts reading the reply.
4. Ask for the latest reviewed conditions and any unresolved points in the assigned workflow. Stop the timer only when they say their result is ready. Ensure both workflows produce the same level of detail: date, venue, set times, total fee, remaining unknown payment date, and a record of the request and clarification questions.
5. Ask what was hard and whether they would try the workflow for another reconstructed scenario. Do not interpret a positive answer as a purchase or as future usage. Note whether the participant needed help and whether the task was completed.

For the baseline, allow their usual combination of message search, notes/template and/or general chatbot; record which tools were actually used and include copying, checking and corrections. For JazzLink, include typing/pasting, waiting while actively operating, inspecting source, fixing errors and saving the output. Distinguish passive model processing and simulated reply wait from hands-on work; record both separately for **both** workflows. Do not exclude recovery/retries or aborts.

Checklist per task:
- [ ] Initial terms and proposed change are distinguished.
- [ ] Questions cover the new total fee and full set times before the reply.
- [ ] No invented agreement, date, time, fee or payment date appears.
- [ ] Final reviewed terms match the provided reply; payment date is still unknown.
- [ ] Person’s review is not presented as venue acknowledgement or a legal signature.
- [ ] Any imported text, model latency, corrections, retries and assistance are included in the appropriate measurements.

For comparable completed tasks with equivalent quality, calculate per participant: `(baseline active seconds - JazzLink active seconds) / baseline active seconds × 100`. If baseline active time is zero, the result is not computable. Do not calculate a winning percentage for incomplete tasks or when the baseline/product deliver different outputs. Present those as incomplete or quality regressions, not exclusions hidden from the report.

Record each task outcome and the exact counts/denominators, order, familiarity, tool choice, help, false confirmations and missed points. With 2–3 people, report individual observations rather than extrapolating a market-wide percentage. A speed gain does not compensate for false confirmation or worse-quality results.
